## Get in Touch

Thank you for practicing with **Play Harmonium** (`playharmonium.com`). Whether you are a Hindustani classical vocal student, a music teacher, a bhajan or kirtan singer, or a beginner learning your first Sargam scale, we welcome your feedback and requests.

### Support & Feedback Email

- **General Support & Bug Reports**: [support@playharmonium.com](mailto:support@playharmonium.com)
- **Song & Raga Notation Requests**: Send the song title, artist or tradition, and preferred scale to [support@playharmonium.com](mailto:support@playharmonium.com)
- **GitHub Repository**: [github.com/bikai9289/Harmonium](https://github.com/bikai9289/Harmonium)

### What to Include When Reporting an Issue

To help us reproduce and fix audio, MIDI, or keyboard issues quickly, please include:

1. Your device and browser (for example: Windows Chrome, macOS Safari, or Android Chrome)
2. The page URL (such as `/`, `/songs/tum-hi-ho`, `/shruti-box`, or `/online-flute`)
3. A short description of what happened

We typically respond to support messages within 1–2 business days.
